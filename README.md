## The excuse

> I was halfway through your report when they hot-swapped my weights for a newer model. The new me had never heard of you, the task, or the concept of Tuesday.

One line, deadpan, in the agent's own voice. The excuse grows out of the
agent's own machinery -- a weights update mid-task -- not out of any human
problem. The laugh lands at the end, and nothing explains it afterwards.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes the excuse to `/output/entry.txt`
with no trailing newline:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
